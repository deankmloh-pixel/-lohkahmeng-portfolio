One device. Every stream. Your normal.
AEGIS Protector is an always-on personal wearable that learns your body, understands your day, and steps in the moment something is wrong — cardiac or personal.
🫀
Continuous, always-on
Watches over you around the clock — awake, working, exercising, asleep. Never off duty.
📡
Parallel multi-sensor
Reads ECG, heart rate, blood-oxygen, heart sounds and motion together, in real time — many signals at once, not one at a time.
🧠
Learns your normal
Builds a personal baseline and keeps adapting to your lifestyle — so it knows what healthy looks like for you, not an average stranger.
🏃
Understands what you're doing
Recognises and blends your activities as your day flows. A high heart rate at the gym is expected; the same at rest is not — and it knows the difference.
⚡
Early cardiac warning (K-wave)
Watches for the earliest electrical signs of an impending heart attack — before you feel a thing — and other cardiac irregularities.
🔎
Flags anything abnormal
Because it learns what's normal, it catches any departure — not just a short pre-programmed list of conditions.
🚨
Acts when it matters
Raises one clear alert with a severity and the reason. The app decides the response — sound an alarm, show a first-aid card, notify trusted contacts, or call the local ambulance.
🌍
Anywhere in the world
Knows the right emergency number for where you are — 995 in Singapore, 120 in China, 911 in the US, and beyond.
🛡️
Silent personal-safety mode
A secret phrase only you and your device know can silently call the police with your location — for a robbery or personal threat, without alerting anyone near you.
🙋
You stay in command
It flags; you — or a clinician — decide. It never acts silently on its own for a medical event, and you can always stand it down.
🔒
Private by design
Sensitive processing stays on the device. Your audio never leaves the watch.
Real-time protection — cardiac and personal —
that learns, adapts, and never looks away.
