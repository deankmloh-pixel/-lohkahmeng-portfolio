The advanced algorithm

AEGIS Protector (AEGIS Protector) is the everyday Lifestyle wearable. AEGIS Clinical is the clinical unlock — an Advanced-AI cardiac engine that runs on the very same device. It learns your personal normal, catches the earliest electrical signs of ischaemia — the K-wave (J/Osborn) — and computes a population-referenced clinical danger score. Enable it, and the lifestyle watch becomes a clinical-grade monitor; nothing new to buy, just a regulated software unlock.

The clinical dial

The Cardiac-Ischaemia NDI is a single 0–100 number, untrained and transparent, that rises steadily from healthy to unwell and reads the same on any device. Four colours — green, amber, orange, red — turn it into something a clinician or a patient can act on at a glance. It is the clinical view; the Lifestyle view keeps everything personal.

Integrates into the hospital desktop

This is where AEGIS Clinical leaves AEGIS Protector behind. The device the patient already wears streams to a hospital desktop, where a single clinician monitors every patient’s heart from one screen — no monitor at every bed, no new hardware. It surfaces only the patients who drift into danger, ranked on the population NDI, so attention goes where it matters. This is the augmenting model: it multiplies one clinician’s reach and cuts the cost of watching, while the clinician confirms every alert.

Lifestyle vs Clinical — the line between them

🫀 AEGIS Protector · Lifestyle
A personal wearable. It learns your heart, judges you against your own baseline, warns you early, and calls for help if you can’t. It lives on your wrist, in your life — it never touches a hospital system. You stay in command.
🏥 AEGIS Clinical
The advanced algorithm, unlocked on the same device — and it integrates into the hospital desktop. The patient’s own wearable feeds one clinician’s screen watching every patient on a population danger scale. Augmenting the ward, not replacing it — home to ward and back.

Why “Clinical”, not just cardiac

AEGIS Clinical is a multi-parameter platform. The heart is the first sense — the same device is built to take more. Next on the roadmap: a non-invasive infrared glucose sensor that adds continuous blood-glucose monitoring, so one wearable watches the heart and metabolic risk together, on one clinical danger scale. (Glucose sensing is a planned integration — designed for, not yet validated here.)

The honest part. Accuracy is measured on physician-labelled public data (PTB-XL + Chapman-Ningbo, PhysioNet CC BY 4.0), patient-independent. “~9 in 10 / ~96 in 100” are catch-rates at a ~5–10% false-alarm rate on early data, rising through continuous monitoring. AEGIS Clinical is a pre-emptive screening & early-warning aid, human-in-command — it does not diagnose or treat and is not a replacement for professional care; clinical features require medical-device clearance in each market.
