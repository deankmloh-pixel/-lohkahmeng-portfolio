The advanced algorithm — P17

AEGIS Protector (P16) is the everyday Lifestyle wearable. P17 is its Clinical option — an Advanced-AI cardiac engine that runs on the very same device. It learns your personal normal, catches the earliest electrical signs of ischaemia — the K-wave (J/Osborn) — and computes a population-referenced clinical danger score. Enable it, and the lifestyle watch becomes a clinical-grade monitor; nothing new to buy, just a regulated software unlock.

Self-learning, adaptive
Builds each wearer’s own baseline and keeps adapting — it judges you against you, not an average stranger.
K-wave early warning
Reads the J/Osborn and ST-T signature of a developing event before symptoms — the pre-emptive window.

How well it works — measured, not claimed

Validated on 66,951 physician-labelled 12-lead ECGs across two independent datasets (PTB-XL and the full 45,152-patient Chapman-Ningbo cohort), always tested on patients it never trained on.

The clinical dial

The Cardiac-Ischaemia NDI is a single 0–100 number, untrained and transparent, that rises steadily from healthy to unwell and reads the same on any device. Four colours — green, amber, orange, red — turn it into something a clinician or a patient can act on at a glance. It is the clinical view; the Lifestyle view keeps everything personal.

Integrates into the hospital desktop

This is where P17 leaves P16 behind. The device the patient already wears streams to a hospital desktop, where a single clinician monitors every patient’s heart from one screen — no monitor at every bed, no new hardware. It surfaces only the patients who drift into danger, ranked on the population NDI, so attention goes where it matters. This is the augmenting model: it multiplies one clinician’s reach and cuts the cost of watching, while the clinician confirms every alert.

P16 vs P17 — the line between them

🫀 P16 · strictly Lifestyle
A personal wearable. It learns your heart, judges you against your own baseline, warns you early, and calls for help if you can’t. It lives on your wrist, in your life — it never touches a hospital system. You stay in command.
🏥 P17 · Clinical
The advanced algorithm, unlocked on the same device — and it integrates into the hospital desktop. The patient’s own wearable feeds one clinician’s screen watching every patient on a population danger scale. Augmenting the ward, not replacing it — home to ward and back.
