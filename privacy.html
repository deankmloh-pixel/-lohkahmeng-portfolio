AEGIS™ Zero Homeless: Privacy Policy

Effective 25 November 2026

AEGIS™ Zero Homeless collects no personal data, uses no internet connection and shares nothing. Everything runs on your device.

AEGIS™ Zero Homeless is an Android app published on Google Play by Aegis Human-AI (Dr Loh Kah Meng, Singapore).

1. No personal data is collected

The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

2. No internet use

The app does not request internet access. All data and calculations are inside the app on your device.

3. Nothing is shared

We do not sell, share or transfer any information, because we receive none.

4. Scenario codes

If you choose to copy a scenario code, it stays on your device until you paste or share it yourself.

5. Children

The app is meant for adults and older students. It does not collect data from anyone.
