🧠AEGIS P21 — PARKINSON AUGMENTING SYSTEM
AEGIS P21 is a validated Parkinson's Disease voice and drawing assessment system built on an 11-engine consensus board. GroupKFold-5 subject-level splitting — no patient leaks across folds. Grey-zone protocol: composite score 35–65% → specialist referral required rather than forced binary verdict. AEGIS scores; a neurologist confirms.

These apps demonstrate clinical AI systems capable of classification, early pre-emptive detection, and progressive augmented management. Because full AEGIS engines and datasets run to several gigabytes, only educational quiz modes are hosted here — the live analysis requires the AEGIS Desktop Application.
