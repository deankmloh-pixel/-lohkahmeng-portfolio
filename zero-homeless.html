AEGIS™ Zero Homeless

A homelessness policy simulator for Android. Pick any US state or one of 187 countries, choose the policies, and watch an index of the conditions linked to homelessness move year by year, from recorded data up to 2024 and an outlook to 2040.

What you can do

Take charge

  • Any of the 50 US states or Washington DC, or any of 187 countries, from a start year of your choice.
  • Ten government policy levers: rental assistance, social and public housing, Housing First, income support, unemployment insurance, health and addiction cover, eviction prevention, support on leaving prison, foster care or hospital, land and zoning, and job programmes in recessions. Each lever has a cost, within a budget.
  • What-if shocks: recession, pandemic, price shock, war or conflict abroad.

Learn from it

  • Ask the built-in AI expert: it searches over a million lever plans for your chosen place and explains the cheapest and the strongest plan that would work.
  • Compare places on a World map and in rankings, by region, sub-region and bloc (ASEAN, BRICS, EU, G7, G20, OECD).
  • See the US president's party by term as context, by party only and as an association, never a cause.

Honest by design

  • Higher index = worse, always. Four colours: Green, Yellow, Orange, Red.
  • Lever strengths come from published studies where they exist; the rest are marked as assumptions.
  • Associations, not causes. Projections are not forecasts.

Private and offline

  • Runs entirely on your device: no account, no internet connection, no ads, no tracking.
  • Nothing you do is sent anywhere. Privacy policy.
  • Best on a 10-inch tablet or a Chromebook; on a phone, turn it sideways.
